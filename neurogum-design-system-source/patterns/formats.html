What each one is for

مستند مسئله — The strongest faceless format. No identity, no on-camera Farsi, no studio — and the most sendable thing you have.

توضیح مکانیزم — The only format where a diagram beats a sentence. Stays technical, never medical.

اثبات اصالت — Runs on real photographs of your own stock or it does not run. Also lives permanently as a Highlight.

هویت — The flatlay lane. Carries desire and self-selects for dollar-adjacent income. No occasion, no numbers.

Entry point → format map
