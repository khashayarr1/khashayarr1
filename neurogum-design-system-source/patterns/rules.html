Rules — do / never

Every rule here traces to a specific section of the playbook. If a design decision is not covered by one of these, it is a judgement call — everything else is settled.

Do

Name one occasion per piece, concretely.
“ساعت ۴ بعدازظهر، سه ساعت کار مانده.” You can film 4pm.

Ink text on every ground.
Including on Brand Blue, where it measures 8.05:1 and looks more expensive than white.

One ground + one accent per carousel.
Ten slides across four grounds reads as a Canva account, not a brand.

Aim crash language at energy drinks and focus pills.
They have no defenders. Tehran café culture does.

Coffee as complement — after, when it is not an option, when it stopped working.
Roughly one format in four.

Design every post to be sent to one named person.
DM sends are the heaviest distribution signal in 2026.

Persian digits in Farsi copy.
ساعت ۴, not “4”. Latin digits only inside Latin phrases.

Own photography, own inventory.
Phase 1 is mood content that is not pretending to be ownership.

Never

تازه / “fresh”
In gum, fresh means breath. One word and the brand collapses back into commodity gum.

سالم / “healthy”
Caffeine cannot carry a health claim, it invites the safety objection, and Instagram suppresses that register outright.

Any generic benefit adjective.
بهتر، تیزتر، بیشتر. Nobody's memory retrieves a product from “I'd like to feel sharper.”

White text on Brand Blue.
2.15:1. Unreadable at feed size. This is the single most common failure.

“به جای قهوه” / instead of coffee.
Substitution framing sets up a test you lose: 40mg versus a 100–150mg americano.

Per-cup price math in content.
It hands the customer the calculator. DM objection-handler only, and only after they raise price first.

Defending the price.
Price is proof of seriousness. Explaining it makes it a cost instead of a signal.

The register of a pill or medicine.
Dosage instructions, health claims, clinical language. Suppressed on Explore and Reels.

Other-app watermarks and AI voiceover.
Both demoted, both read as drop-shipping.

“لینک در بایو”.
The landing page is a fallback. The DM is the store.

Justified or centred Farsi paragraphs.
RTL justification wrecks kashida joins. Right-align, ragged left.

Borrowed flatlays.
The brand-fluent audience you are targeting is exactly the audience trained to spot a repost.

The filter above all of them

Anonymous-first. No face, no name, no identity on the page — and every design decision passes through that before anything else. It is worth more now than when it was written.
